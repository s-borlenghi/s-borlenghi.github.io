About me

I bring together two worlds that often stay apart: training neural networks and building the software that actually puts them into production.

I'm a Software Engineer with five years of experience at Giunti Psychometrics. Python is my main language for AI and backend work; on the web I use JavaScript, Node.js and React.

My goal is to turn an experimental model into a scalable product people actually use, balancing technical efficiency with business goals. Quality comes first: every release gets tested, every anomaly gets understood down to its root cause.

What I bring to a team

Skills that reinforce each other across the whole lifecycle of an AI-based product.

AI and deep learning with PyTorch

I design and train neural networks for problems that hand-written rules can't solve: training from scratch, fine-tuning and experimentation. I own the whole cycle, from data preparation and result validation to integration into applications.

Full stack and architecture

Applications and microservices across the whole stack: React and Next.js on the frontend, FastAPI, Django, Flask or Node.js on the backend, data in SQL.

QA and bug hunting

End-to-end tests with Playwright, API tests with Postman, regression checks. I also diagnose model-specific issues: overfitting, data leakage, non-reproducible results.

Automation and tooling

Python scripts, dashboards and internal tools that shorten release cycles and reduce technical debt.

Technical support and stakeholders

Checking that APIs and integrations meet requirements, and fixing critical bugs in Scrum teams alongside PMs and POs.
